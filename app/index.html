Expired Drops & Protocol Settlements

When a drop expires, anyone can finalize it: the unclaimed tokens are split 90% to the drop creator and 10% to the protocol treasury (0xA125...793). Finalizing does not send tokens to your wallet. It only credits them to a pending balance in the vault, and each party must then withdraw their own pending balance in a second transaction.
